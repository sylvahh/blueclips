Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy covers Blue Clips Poster, a private tool used only by the owner of the Blue Clips channels (@blueclipshq) to publish short videos to their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

Blue Clips Poster uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos, set video thumbnails and edit video details on the signed-in YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to that channel's videos and their public statistics (such as view counts).

How the data is used

The tool uploads the owner's own Shorts and reads their view counts to track performance. It does not access other users' data, comments, messages or subscribers.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No cookies or analytics are used on this website.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's Third-party apps & services page. Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored data.
